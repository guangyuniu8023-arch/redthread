# Redthread Privacy Policy

Revision date: November 6, 2026

Redthread is a skills-only plugin. It does not operate an external server, create user accounts, or independently collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The workflow sends the photos, paper references, and prompts needed for generation to the user's configured Seedream 5.0 Pro service. If optional external segmentation is used, that service receives the photo needed to extract the subject. These services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Working assets stay in the active task workspace or temporary storage. The agent shows the finished collage by default and may show specific intermediate results when the user explicitly requests them. API credentials must remain in secure user configuration and must not appear in prompts, logs, or the Skill package.

Processing by the host product and operating system remains governed by their respective terms and privacy policies. This policy covers the Seedream revision maintained at [guangyuniu8023-arch/redthread](https://github.com/guangyuniu8023-arch/redthread).

Questions may be submitted through the repository support channel described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
